AWAITING YOUR CURIOSITY

A root is an x-value where f(x) = 0. Find every distinct real root, even when the curve only touches the axis.

    WHAT TO NOTICE

    A cubic can cross the axis three times. Follow this curve from left to right, then solve to locate each crossing.

    Explore the mathematics ↗
    01

    A root can cross. Or just touch.

    At a real root, the polynomial is zero. Some roots cross the horizontal axis; others turn around there. Repeated roots appear once in the results.

    02

    Count first. Then look closer.

    Geode uses exact arithmetic and a Sturm sequence to count and isolate real roots. Checked Geode refinement and bisection then narrow down their locations.

    03

    More digits. A smaller error.

    Requesting 30 accuracy digits asks for absolute error at most 10−30. MPFR supplies the precision; the graph uses ordinary floating-point numbers and can miss fine detail.

    10−30
    ONE IMPORTANT DETAIL

    The accuracy bound applies to the polynomial’s stored binary coefficients. Decimal inputs are rounded at your chosen working precision, so very sensitive or repeated roots can change. Higher precision preserves more of what you enter.
